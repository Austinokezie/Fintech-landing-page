Can I change my plan later?

Yes, you can upgrade, downgrade, or change your plan at any time through your dashboard settings. Changes are effective immediately or at the start of your next billing cycle, depending on the modification.

How do I change my account email?

You can update your registered email address in the 'Account Settings' section of your profile. For security, you may need to confirm the change via a verification link sent to both the old and new email addresses.

Is your payment platform secure?

Absolutely. We use industry-leading encryption and partner with certified payment processors (e.g., Stripe, PayPal) to ensure all your transaction details are handled securely and are PCI compliant.

Can other info added to an invoice?

Yes, you can specify custom information, such as a PO number, VAT ID, or project reference, to be included on your invoices. This can typically be managed in your billing preferences.

How does billing work?

Billing is handled on a monthly or annual subscription basis, depending on your chosen plan. You will be charged automatically on your renewal date, and invoices are available immediately in your account portal.

What is your cancellation policy?

You can cancel your subscription at any time. Your service will remain active until the end of the current paid billing period, and you will not be charged again.
